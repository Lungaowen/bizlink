BizLink landing page

Stock moves together, costs come down.

BizLink bundles your restock with other shops on your street, then one driver collects and delivers it all — for a fraction of a solo courier run.

The problem

  • High delivery costs. Courier and ride-hailing fares eat into thin margins.
  • Unreliable timing. Restocks arrive late, or not at all.
  • No online reach. Customers outside walking distance never find the shop.

What BizLink does

  • Bulk collection. One driver, one trip, several shops' stock.
  • Scheduled restocks. Fixed daily or weekly slots — no more running out.
  • Geotagged addressing. "Next to Boxer, near the big tree" is a valid address.
